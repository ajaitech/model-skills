# World-Class SEO Skill

This skill provides a methodology for achieving top-tier search engine rankings. It is designed for a full-stack architect and emphasizes a holistic, data-driven approach to SEO.

## The SEO Methodology

Follow these phases to implement a world-class SEO strategy.

### Phase 1: Foundational & Technical SEO

1.  **Create `robots.txt`**:
    *   Use the `references/robots-template.txt` as a starting point.
    *   Customize it to allow crawlers access to all important content while blocking access to private or irrelevant sections.
    *   Add a reference to your `sitemap.xml` file.

2.  **Create `sitemap.xml`**:
    *   Use the `references/sitemap-template.xml` as a template.
    *   Include all canonical URLs for your site.
    *   Keep it updated as you add or remove content.

3.  **Implement Structured Data (Schema Markup)**:
    *   Use JSON-LD to add structured data to your pages.
    *   Refer to `references/structured-data-examples.json` for common schemas like `Article`, `Product`, and `Organization`.
    *   Use Google's Rich Results Test to validate your implementation.

4.  **Core Web Vitals & Page Speed**:
    *   Ensure your site is fast, responsive, and stable.
    *   Optimize images, leverage browser caching, and minimize render-blocking resources.

### Phase 2: On-Page SEO & Content Strategy

1.  **Keyword Research & Content Strategy**:
    *   Identify your target audience and the keywords they use.
    *   Create high-quality, authoritative content that provides real value to your users.
    *   Focus on semantic depth and covering topics comprehensively.

2.  **On-Page Optimization**:
    *   Use your target keywords naturally in titles, headings, meta descriptions, and content.
    *   Use descriptive URLs and internal linking to create a logical site structure.

### Phase 3: Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

1.  **Optimize for AI-driven Search**:
    *   Structure your content into self-contained, easily extractable "blocks" or "chunks" that AI engines can use to generate answers.
    *   Focus on providing proprietary data, original research, and expert analysis that AI models will cite.

### Phase 4: Off-Page SEO & Authority Building

1.  **Link Building**:
    *   Acquire high-quality backlinks from reputable and relevant websites.
2.  **Social Signals**:
    *   Promote your content on social media to generate social signals, which are particularly important for Bing.

## Bing-Specific Optimizations

*   **Exact-Match Keywords**: Bing's algorithm is more literal than Google's. Use exact-match keywords in titles and headings.
*   **IndexNow Protocol**: Use the IndexNow API to instantly index new or updated content on Bing.

By following this comprehensive methodology, you will be able to achieve sustainable, top-tier rankings in all major search engines.
